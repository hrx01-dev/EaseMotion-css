Animated Material Badge

A compact status system using Material-inspired elevation, clear color meaning, responsive wrapping, and restrained entrance motion.

STATUS SYSTEM

Current states

Use short labels to communicate state without making the badge carry too much information.

New Verified In review Pending

IMPLEMENTATION

Design notes

01

Elevation

Small shadows establish a clear layer above the surface without creating excessive visual weight.

02

Motion

Entrance movement is short and subtle so status changes remain informative rather than distracting.

03

Responsive

Badges wrap naturally and preserve their touch-friendly spacing on smaller screens.

REUSE

Usage guidance

  1. Keep badge labels concise and meaningful.
  2. Do not rely on color alone to communicate critical information.
  3. Keep motion optional for users who prefer reduced movement.
